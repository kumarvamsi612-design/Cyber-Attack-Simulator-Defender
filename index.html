THREAT EVOLUTION

🧬 Evolution of Cyber Attacks

Cyber attacks have evolved from simple worms to highly sophisticated, automated and AI-assisted threats.

1988 🪱 Internet Worms

Early attacks focused on automatic network propagation.

2000 📧 Email Malware

Attackers used email and social engineering to reach millions of users.

2010 🏭 Industrial Malware

Malware began targeting physical industrial systems.

2017 💰 Ransomware

Criminal groups increasingly used encryption for financial extortion.

2020 🔗 Supply Chain Attacks

Attackers compromised trusted software and service providers.

2024+ 🤖 AI-Assisted Threats

AI can increase the scale and realism of phishing and other cyber attacks.
